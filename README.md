## Storage and Preservation of Red Sumatra Kratom

Proper storage techniques ensure Red Sumatra Kratom maintains its potency and quality over extended periods. Understanding optimal storage conditions prevents degradation and contamination.

### Optimal Storage Conditions

**Temperature Control**: Store kratom in cool, stable temperatures between 60-70°F. Avoid areas with temperature fluctuations like near heating vents or windows.

**Humidity Management**: Maintain low humidity levels below 50% to prevent mold growth and alkaloid degradation. Use desiccant packets in humid climates.

**Light Protection**: Keep kratom away from direct sunlight and UV exposure, which can break down alkaloids and reduce potency.

**Air Exposure**: Minimize oxygen contact by using airtight containers and avoiding frequent opening of storage containers.

### Container Selection

Different storage containers offer various advantages:

**Glass Jars**: Excellent for long-term storage, non-reactive, easy to clean
**Mylar Bags**: Superior moisture barrier, flexible storage, good for bulk quantities  
**Vacuum Sealed Bags**: Maximum freshness preservation, space-efficient
**Food-Grade Buckets**: Ideal for large quantities, stackable, pest-resistant

### Signs of Kratom Degradation

Watch for these indicators that Red Sumatra Kratom quality has declined:

🚨 **Color changes** from reddish-brown to gray or yellow
🚨 **Musty or off odors** indicating mold or bacterial growth  
🚨 **Clumping or caking** suggesting moisture exposure
🚨 **Reduced effects** at normal dosages
🚨 **Visible mold or foreign materials**
